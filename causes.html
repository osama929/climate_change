01 • CAUSES

What is driving climate change?

Earth's climate changes naturally, but today's rapid warming is primarily driven by human activities.

🏭

Burning fossil fuels

Coal, oil and natural gas are burned for electricity, transport and industry. This releases carbon dioxide and other greenhouse gases.

🌳

Deforestation

Forests store carbon. Clearing and degrading them releases stored carbon while reducing an important natural carbon sink.

🐄

Agriculture

Livestock, rice production, fertilizers and land-use changes contribute methane, nitrous oxide and carbon dioxide emissions.

🏗️

Industry & consumption

Manufacturing materials such as cement and steel, as well as waste and energy-intensive consumption, add greenhouse gas emissions.

The greenhouse effect

Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared radiation. The natural greenhouse effect makes Earth habitable; increasing greenhouse gas concentrations strengthen it and warm the planet.
