02 • EFFECTS

How is climate change affecting the world?

Climate risks are connected: changes in temperature and rainfall can cascade through ecosystems, infrastructure, food systems and communities.

🔥

Extreme heat

Hotter conditions and more frequent or intense heat extremes can increase risks to health, agriculture and infrastructure.

🌧️

Changing rainfall

Some regions experience heavier rainfall and flooding while others face longer or more severe droughts.

🧊

Melting ice

Warming contributes to glacier and ice-sheet loss and reduces sea ice, affecting ecosystems and contributing to sea-level rise.

🐠

Ecosystems

Species can shift their ranges and seasonal behavior. Rapid environmental change can increase pressure on vulnerable ecosystems.

🏠

Communities

Climate hazards can damage homes, roads and services and can increase economic and social pressures.

🍚

Food & water

Heat, droughts, floods and changing seasons can affect crop yields and water availability in vulnerable areas.
