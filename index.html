THE BIG PICTURE

Why should we care?

Climate change influences weather, ecosystems, food, water, health, cities and economies. Understanding the science helps us make better decisions.

🌡️

A warming world

Human activities have increased greenhouse gas concentrations, trapping more heat in Earth's climate system.

🌊

Changing environments

Sea levels, rainfall patterns, ice cover and ecosystems are changing as the planet warms.

🌱

Room for action

Cutting emissions and adapting to unavoidable changes can reduce risks and protect communities.
