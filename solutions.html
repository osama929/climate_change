03 • SOLUTIONS

What can we do?

Climate action combines mitigation—reducing greenhouse gas emissions—with adaptation—reducing harm from climate impacts.

☀️

Clean energy

Expand renewable energy, improve energy efficiency and modernize electricity systems to reduce dependence on fossil fuels.

🚲

Smarter transport

Public transport, walking, cycling, efficient vehicles and cleaner fuels can reduce emissions from mobility.

🌲

Protect nature

Protecting and restoring forests, wetlands and other ecosystems can support biodiversity and carbon storage.

🏙️

Adapt communities

Early-warning systems, resilient infrastructure, heat planning and water management can reduce climate risks.

Small choices matter—but systems matter too.

Individual actions can help, especially when combined with policies, investment, innovation and collective action that change the systems producing emissions.
